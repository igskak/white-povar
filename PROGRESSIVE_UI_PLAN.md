# White Povar: progressive UI plan

Each item is delivered as a self-contained commit so it can be reviewed,
measured, or reverted independently. Motion must respect reduced-motion
preferences, and every interaction must remain usable with touch and keyboard.

1. **Premium collection showcase** — replace the generic course gate with an
   editorial module that previews real collection dishes, states the value and
   item count, and adapts its CTA for locked and entitled users.
2. **Shared recipe transition** — carry the selected recipe image from its card
   into the detail hero while preserving navigation and scroll context.
3. **Editorial section reveals** — add restrained, one-time entrance motion to
   the hero and Premium module without scroll-jacking.
4. **Responsive recipe-card interactions** — give pointer users richer image,
   metadata, and arrow feedback while keeping touch cards direct and stable.
5. **Personalized shelves** — introduce useful modules such as “Continue”,
   “Ready in 30 minutes”, and “From your saved recipes”, based on real signals.
6. **Sticky discovery bar** — surface search, filters, scanning, and Premium in
   a compact contextual bar after the main hero leaves the viewport.
7. **Action microfeedback** — refine save, unlock, and collection-progress
   feedback with accessible state changes and short, purposeful motion.
